System Architecture

How CHIRANJEEVI works

A multi-layer AI pipeline converting raw sensor streams into
actionable clinical insights in under 2 seconds — on-device, offline-capable.

Swipe to see technical flowchart →
01

Sensor Acquisition

Simultaneous capture from RGB camera (30fps), Ti mmWave radar (FMCW), and FLIR thermal module. Edge-synced at hardware level.

30fps rPPG77GHz FMCWFLIR Lepton
02

Signal Processing

Per-modality DSP pipelines: Euler video magnification for rPPG, range-Doppler FFT for radar, thermal segmentation for surface mapping.

Euler MagnificationFFT PipelineThermal Seg.

AI Inference Engine

Lightweight transformer models fused across modalities. Runs on-device via ONNX Runtime — no cloud dependency for core vitals.

Roadmap & Vision

The Future MVP

Scaling CHIRANJEEVI from kiosk to everywhere.

Current Applications

  • Can be deployed in govt/private hospitals as a chamber with a kiosk near the entrance for a quick vitals scan before OPD consultations.
  • Can be deployed in heavy public areas (railways, airports) as a health diagnosis chamber with an AI agent for medical guidance.
  • Ideal for remote areas where hospitals are far, enabling daily preventive body checkups.
  • Can be installed at homes for immediate preventive screening. (Act before consequence)

Future Goals

  • Improving precision with strictly clinical-grade hardware devices.
  • Develop a companion app to support clinical video consultations and manage digital reports.
  • Integrate RIS technology for accurate fluid density detection.
  • Add a bone densitometer and SpO2 hardware for expanding the biomarker taxonomy.
  • Introduce advanced infrared cameras and a bottom-mounted BMI scale in the screening chamber.
